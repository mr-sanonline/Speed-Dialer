Speed Dialer
One-page guide for the tele-calling team
1 · OPEN IT ONCE

Open mr-sanonline.github.io/Speed-Dialer in Chrome or Safari. Nothing to install.

Then add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:

  • iPhone — Share button, then Add to Home Screen
  • Android — ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen
2 · SIGN IN

Tap your name under your team. Your leads load automatically — you only see the ones assigned to you, so nobody double-calls.

You stay signed in. Closing the browser is fine; you come back where you left off.

3 · MAKE A CALL
TAP THE GREEN BUTTON

The number dials straight away. No typing.

PICK THE OUTCOME

When you come back, choose Interested, Call back later, No answer, Switched off, or Not interested.

FILL WHAT YOU LEARNED

Location, property type, configuration, budget, project, timeline, follow-up date, language, notes. All optional.

SAVE

Goes into the master sheet by itself. Tap Next ↓ for the next uncalled lead.

GOOD TO KNOW
  • Always pick an outcome. A call without one doesn't count towards your 200 and shows up as "attempted, not logged".
  • Notes: one line per point. Each line becomes its own bullet.
  • Configuration is free text — 2BHK, 30x40, 2 acres, whatever fits.
  • DUPE badge means that number is in the sheet twice. Call once, then move on.
  • WhatsApp button opens a chat with a ready message.
THE DOT AT THE BOTTOM
Green — everything saved to the sheet.
Amber — saves waiting, usually weak signal. Keep calling; nothing is lost. It sends itself when signal returns, even if you close the app.
Red — something's wrong. Tell your manager; your work is still saved on the phone.
YOUR DAY

The Today tab shows calls against your 200, outcomes, interested leads captured, and how many are left. Resets at midnight.
